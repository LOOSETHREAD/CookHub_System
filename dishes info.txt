Dinuguan
Description: A rich and savory Filipino stew made with pork and pig's blood, flavored with vinegar and spices, often served with rice or puto (rice cakes).

Procedure:

Sauté: In a pot, heat 2 tbsp of oil. Sauté 5 cloves of minced garlic and 1 chopped onion until fragrant.
Add Pork: Add 1 kg of diced pork and cook until browned.
Add Vinegar: Pour in 1/2 cup of vinegar and cook for 5 minutes without stirring.
Add Blood: Stir in 1 cup of pork blood and 1 cup of water. Simmer for 30-40 minutes until thickened.
Season: Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot with rice or puto.
Ingredients:

Pork (1 kg): PHP 200-250
Pork blood (1 cup): PHP 30-50
Vinegar: PHP 5-10
Garlic and onion: PHP 20-30
Total Cost: PHP 255-340

7. Escabeche
Description: A Filipino sweet and sour fish dish where fried fish is topped with a colorful sauce made from vinegar, sugar, and vegetables.

Procedure:

Fry Fish: Season 1 kg of fish with salt and pepper. Fry until golden brown and set aside.
Sauté: In a pan, heat 2 tbsp of oil. Sauté 5 cloves of minced garlic, 1 chopped onion, and 1 thumb-sized piece of minced ginger until fragrant.
Add Vegetables: Add 2 sliced bell peppers and 1 sliced carrot. Cook for 5 minutes.
Make Sauce: Pour in a mixture of 1/2 cup vinegar, 1/2 cup water, and 2 tbsp sugar. Simmer for 10 minutes.
Combine: Pour the sauce over the fried fish and simmer for another 5 minutes.
Serve: Serve hot with rice.
Ingredients:

Fish (1 kg): PHP 150-200
Bell peppers (2 pcs): PHP 30-40
Carrots (1 pc): PHP 10-15
Vinegar, sugar, water: PHP 10-15
Garlic, onion, ginger: PHP 20-30
Total Cost: PHP 220-300

8. Humba
Description: A Visayan braised pork dish with a sweet and savory flavor profile, made with pork belly, black beans, pineapple, and soy sauce.

Procedure:

Sauté: In a pot, heat 2 tbsp of oil. Sauté 5 cloves of minced garlic and 1 chopped onion until fragrant.
Add Pork: Add 1 kg of pork belly and cook until browned.
Add Ingredients: Stir in 1/2 cup of black beans, 1 can of pineapple chunks with juice, 1/2 cup of soy sauce, and 2 tbsp of brown sugar.
Simmer: Reduce heat and simmer for 1-1.5 hours until the pork is tender and the sauce is thickened.
Season: Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot with rice.
Ingredients:

Pork belly (1 kg): PHP 250-300
Black beans (1/2 cup): PHP 10-15
Pineapple chunks (1 can): PHP 20-30
Soy sauce, brown sugar: PHP 20-30
Garlic and onion: PHP 20-30
Total Cost: PHP 320-405

9. Kare Kare
Description: A rich Filipino stew made with oxtail, tripe, and vegetables in a thick peanut sauce, traditionally served with bagoong (shrimp paste).

Procedure:

Boil Meat: Boil 1 kg of oxtail and tripe until tender, then set aside.
Make Peanut Sauce: In a pot, sauté 5 cloves of minced garlic and 1 chopped onion. Add 1/2 cup of peanut butter and 1/2 cup of annatto water, stir well.
Combine: Add the boiled meat and simmer for 30 minutes.
Add Vegetables: Stir in 1 bunch of pechay, 1 eggplant (sliced), and 1 banana blossom (sliced). Cook until vegetables are tender.
Serve: Serve hot with rice and a side of bagoong.
Ingredients:

Oxtail and tripe (1 kg): PHP 300-400
Peanut butter: PHP 20-30
Annatto water: PHP 10-15
Pechay, eggplant, banana blossom: PHP 30-40
Garlic and onion: PHP 20-30
Total Cost: PHP 380-515

10. Kinilaw
Description: A Filipino dish of raw fish marinated in vinegar and citrus juices, mixed with onions, ginger, and chili peppers, often compared to ceviche.

Procedure:

Prepare Fish: Clean and slice 500 g of fresh fish into bite-sized pieces.
Marinate: In a bowl, combine the fish, juice of 5 calamansi, 1/2 cup of vinegar, 1 thumb-sized piece of minced ginger, 1 chopped onion, and 2 chopped chili peppers. Mix well.
Chill: Let it sit for at least 30 minutes in the refrigerator to marinate.
Serve: Serve chilled as an appetizer or main dish.
Ingredients:

Fresh fish (500 g): PHP 100-150
Vinegar and calamansi: PHP 15-25
Ginger, onion, chili peppers: PHP 20-30
Total Cost: PHP 135-205

13. Lugaw
Description: A simple and comforting Filipino rice porridge, often cooked with ginger and garlic, and topped with various ingredients like boiled egg or chicken.

Procedure:

Ingredients:

Rice (1 cup): PHP 20-30

24. Tapsilog
Description: A Filipino breakfast dish consisting of marinated beef (tapa), fried rice (sinangag), and a fried egg (itlog).

Procedure:

Marinate Beef: Marinate 500 g of beef slices in 1/2 cup soy sauce, juice of 5 calamansi, and 5 cloves of minced garlic for at least 30 minutes.
Cook Beef: In a pan, heat 2 tbsp of oil and cook the marinated beef until tender and caramelized.
Cook Rice: In a separate pan, heat 2 tbsp of oil and fry 3 cups of cooked rice with 5 cloves of minced garlic.
Fry Eggs: Fry 4 eggs sunny side up.
Serve: Serve the beef tapa, garlic rice, and fried eggs together on a plate.
Ingredients:

Beef (500 g): PHP 200-250
Soy sauce and calamansi: PHP 20-30
Garlic: PHP 10-15
Eggs (4 pcs): PHP 20-30
Rice: PHP 20-30
Total Cost: PHP 270-355
